# Treino Misto: Lista 2 · Gabarito

**Voltar à lista:** [[Treino Misto - Lista 2]]

**1.** $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3} - 1 = 0$
**2.** $0{,}1\overline{6} = \frac{16 - 1}{90} = \frac{1}{6}$; $\;\frac{1}{6} \cdot 6 = 1$
**3.** $\frac{4}{9} \div \frac{2}{9} = 2$
**4.** $\frac{1}{4} + \frac{3}{4} = 1$
**5.** $(-2)^{-3} = \frac{1}{(-2)^3} = -\frac{1}{8}$; $\;-\frac{1}{8} \cdot 16 = -2$
**6.** $0{,}6 - 0{,}3 = 0{,}3 = \frac{3}{10}$
**7.** $1{,}\overline{7} = 1 + \frac{7}{9} = \frac{16}{9}$; $\;\sqrt{\frac{16}{9}} = \frac{4}{3}$
**8.** $0{,}5 = \frac{1}{2}$, então $\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-2} = 4$; $\;4 - 2 = 2$
**9.** $1{,}5 + 2{,}25 = 3{,}75 = \frac{15}{4}$
**10.** $0{,}\overline{27} = \frac{27}{99} = \frac{3}{11}$; $\;\frac{3}{11} \cdot 11 = 3$
**11.** $3 - (\sqrt[3]{8})^2 + \frac{1}{9} \cdot 9 = 3 - 4 + 1 = 0$
**12.** $\sqrt{8 \cdot 0{,}5} = \sqrt{4} = 2$
**13.** $\sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3}$, então $(\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{3})^2 = (-\sqrt{3})^2 = 3$
**14.** $(0{,}01)^{-1/2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{0{,}01}} = \frac{1}{0{,}1} = 10$; $\;\sqrt[3]{1000} = 10$; $\;10 - 10 = 0$
**15.** $\sqrt{\frac{4}{9}} = \frac{2}{3}$; $\;\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{-1} = \frac{3}{2}$; $\;0{,}1\overline{6} = \frac{1}{6}$. Então $\frac{4}{6} + \frac{9}{6} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{12}{6} = 2$
