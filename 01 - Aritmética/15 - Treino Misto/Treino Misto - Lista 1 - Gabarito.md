# Treino Misto: Lista 1 · Gabarito

**Voltar à lista:** [[Treino Misto - Lista 1]]

**1.** $\frac{-9 + 10}{12} = \frac{1}{12}$
**2.** $\frac{2}{8} - \frac{7}{8} = -\frac{5}{8}$
**3.** $-\frac{18}{30} = -\frac{3}{5}$
**4.** $-\frac{6}{5} \cdot \left(-\frac{5}{3}\right) = 2$
**5.** $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{4} - \frac{5}{4} = \frac{2 + 3 - 5}{4} = 0$
**6.** $-\frac{2}{5} \div \left(-\frac{2}{5}\right) = 1$
**7.** $-\frac{1}{6} \cdot 6 = -1$
**8.** $\frac{3}{2} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{5}{6}$; $\;\frac{5}{6} \cdot \left(-\frac{12}{5}\right) = -2$
**9.** $\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2} = -\frac{1}{4}$; $\;2 - 3 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) = 2 + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}$
**10.** $0{,}1 + 0{,}3 = 0{,}4 = \frac{2}{5}$
**11.** $\frac{5}{8} + \frac{3}{8} = 1$; $\;1 \div \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) = -4$
**12.** $\frac{4}{-0{,}8} = -5$; $\;\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) \cdot (-6) = 3$; $\;-5 - 3 = -8$
**13.** $1 - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$; $\;\frac{5}{6} \cdot \frac{12}{5} = 2$
**14.** $\frac{-3}{3/4} = -4$; $\;0{,}6 \cdot 5 = 3$; $\;-4 + 3 = -1$
**15.** $-\frac{3 + 2 + 1}{6} = -1$; $\;(-1) \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{8}\right) = \frac{1}{8}$
