# Treino Misto: Lista 3 · Gabarito

**Voltar à lista:** [[Treino Misto - Lista 3]]

**1.** $\left[\frac{1}{4} + \frac{3}{4}\right] \cdot (-4) + 0{,}4 \cdot 5 = -4 + 2 = $ **−2**
**2.** $\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{2} = -\frac{1}{6}$; $\;-\frac{1}{6} \div \left(-\frac{1}{6}\right) = 1$; $\;1 + \frac{1}{2} = $ **3/2**
**3.** $(0{,}5)^2 \cdot 4 = 1$; $\;\frac{1}{3} + 1 = \frac{4}{3}$; $\;\frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 1$; $\;2 - 1 = 1$; $\;1 \div 0{,}25 = $ **4**
**4.** $\frac{3\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}} = 1$; $\;(-1)^{2026} = 1$; $\;0{,}\overline{9} = 1$. Então $1 - 1 + 1 = $ **1**
**5.** $\frac{9}{4} - \frac{5}{4} = 1$; $\;(0{,}\overline{4})^{1/2} = \sqrt{\frac{4}{9}} = \frac{2}{3}$; $\;1 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = $ **2/3**
**6.** $1 - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{8}{9}$; $\;\frac{8}{9} \cdot \frac{9}{8} = 1$; $\;1 \div \frac{1}{4} = $ **4**
**7.** $\frac{\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6}}{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{1}{6}} = $ **3**
**8.** $\sqrt{9} = 3$; $\;3 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 2$; $\;\sqrt[3]{-\frac{27}{8}} = -\frac{3}{2}$; $\;2 - \left(-\frac{3}{2}\right) = $ **7/2**
**9.** $\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^{-1} = 5$; $\;5 \cdot 0{,}08 = 0{,}4$; $\;-0{,}2 + 0{,}4 = 0{,}2$; $\;0{,}2^{-1} = \frac{1}{0{,}2} = $ **5**
**10.** $1{,}\overline{2} - 0{,}\overline{2} = 1$; $\;\sqrt{\frac{1}{0{,}25}} = \sqrt{4} = 2$; $\;(-8) \div 4 = -2$; $\;1 \cdot 2 - 2 = $ **0**
**11.** Tudo em base 2: $\frac{2^{10} \cdot 2^{-6}}{2^{6} \cdot 2^{-3}} = \frac{2^4}{2^3} = $ **2**
**12.** $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{3} = 1$; $\;1 \cdot 9 - 9 = 0$; $\;0 \cdot 7 = 0$; $\;\sqrt{0{,}0001} = 0{,}01$; $\;0{,}01 \cdot 100 = 1$. Total: **1**
